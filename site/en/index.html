Story

Barış Kılıçarslan

Barış Kılıçarslan began his studies in engineering and completed them in business administration. At the headquarters of Bilkent Sport International he worked in the Customer Relations and Public Relations departments, also in management roles.

For six years he worked with Royal Caribbean International in 24 countries across North America, South America and the Caribbean, in customer relations, food and beverage, and sales, including in management roles. In Miami he completed the train-the-trainer program of the Radiance of the Seas Royal Training Academy and began working as a trainer.

Back in Turkey, he chose face-to-face sales, real estate sales and motivation as his fields and began working with RE/MAX Türkiye and İzgören Akademi. He completed DVK with Ahmet Şerif İzgören, UPW with Anthony Robbins, BTS with Jack Canfield and the International NLP Practitioner training with NLP co-founder Richard Bandler.

He has presented in Turkey, North America, Austria, Germany, Greece, Argentina and Romania. With more than 1,900 days of training and seminars he has worked with hundreds of organizations, and over 200,000 people have attended his sessions. He speaks in Turkish and English on sales, motivation, real estate consulting and luxury home sales. Participant satisfaction in his programs is 97.4%.
